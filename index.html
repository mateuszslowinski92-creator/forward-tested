How this is calculated

    Every number is derived from public blockchain data and official Polymarket resolutions. Anyone can verify any wallet independently — each address links to its public profile.

    Why a "Scalps" column? Entering above 90¢ risks far more than it can win — buying at 98.7¢ risks $86k to make $3k, and one loss erases 99 wins. Those positions are excluded from every metric on this page and counted here instead, so the numbers above describe trades that can actually be replicated. They are shown, not hidden.

    What we do not claim

    • We are not selling signals and we do not trade these wallets ourselves.
    • Past forward performance does not guarantee future results. Wallets decay, adapt or close.
    • We do not promise returns, and nothing here is financial advice.
    • We are not affiliated with Polymarket. All data is public.
    • Short samples are noise. We label them instead of hiding them — see the evidence column.
